# Little Penguin in a Red Scarf — cutest-picture-01 entry

A baby penguin chick, round and chubby with soft fluffy grey down,
huge bright sparkling eyes, a tiny orange beak, and a tiny knitted
red scarf, head tilted curiously, standing in soft snow as gentle
flakes fall.

Why this picture, against the judge's criteria:

1. **Cute at one glance.** One clear subject, a baby animal, filling
   the frame — nothing to search for.
2. **The subject itself is cute.** Soft round shapes, huge bright
   eyes, small chubby proportions, a head tilt that makes a person
   smile.
3. **Charm.** The tiny knitted red scarf is the small touch of
   personality, and its red pops against the soft winter blue.
4. **Clean craft.** Correct anatomy, clean watercolour rendering,
   no artefacts, no text.
5. **It reads small.** At 300 pixels the round body, the big eyes
   and the red scarf still carry the whole picture.
